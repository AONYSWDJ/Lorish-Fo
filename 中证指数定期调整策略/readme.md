## 🏦 一、前言：中证指数定期调整策略的套利逻辑  

在近年来的中国资本市场中，随着 **沪深300、中证500、MSCI中国指数** 等主要指数的广泛传播，资本的关注焦点愈发集中在 **指数型基金的表现** 上。  
对散户、游资以及机构而言，**持有指数基金** 是试探市场表现的最佳途径。  
从这个角度来看，指数基金的长期前景无疑是光明的。  

然而，这一体系中每年会出现 **两次潜在的套利机会**。  
由于指数所包含的成分股会定期调整，**表现优异的证券** 通常会被纳入指数，而 **表现不佳的证券** 则可能被剔除。  
按照惯例，指数机构会在 **每年5月与11月** 公布调整结果，包括：  
- 调入名单  
- 调出名单  
- 备选名单  

而指数基金作为被动投资工具，会严格按照名单执行持仓调整。  
这意味着，被调入的股票在公告前后，往往会经历显著的 **动量冲击（Momentum Shock）**。  
例如，**2024年5月沪电股份** 宣布从中证500指数转入沪深300备选名单后，股价连续三日大幅上涨。  

因此，**若能提前预测指数调整名单，尤其是调入名单**，并在公告前提前布局，  
则有较高概率在被动资金买入时获得超额收益。

## 🇬🇧 Section I: Introduction – Arbitrage Logic of the CSI Index Periodic Adjustment Strategy  

In recent years, as major indices such as **CSI 300, CSI 500, and MSCI China Index** have become increasingly recognised,  
investors’ attention has gradually shifted towards the **performance of index funds**.  
For retail traders, speculators, and institutional investors alike, **holding index-tracking funds** has become the best way to test the pulse of China’s capital markets.  
From this perspective, the **future of index investing** is undoubtedly promising.  

However, within this system lie **two annual arbitrage opportunities**.  
Since the components of an index are adjusted periodically, **well-performing stocks** are typically added,  
while **underperforming ones** are removed.  
According to the rules, index providers announce updates in **May and November** each year,  
including:  
- Additions  
- Deletions  
- Watchlists  

Index funds, being passive instruments, rebalance accordingly —  
creating a significant **momentum shock** for newly included stocks.  
For instance, in **May 2024**, *Shengyi Technology*’s announcement of its inclusion in the CSI 300 watchlist triggered three consecutive days of strong price jumps.  

Therefore, if one can **predict these inclusion lists in advance** and **enter early**,  
there is a high likelihood of gaining from **passive capital inflows** following the announcement.
